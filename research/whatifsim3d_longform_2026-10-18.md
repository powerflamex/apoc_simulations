What works:
- One repeatable formula: a single rule that keeps escalating ("...and couldn't stop?") with a big live counter.
- Thumbnail = the counter itself: one huge number + a comparison anyone gets ("hotter than the Sun", "colder than space").
- "You" beats "Earth": You-hotter 79K vs Earth-hotter 49K (same 9,900°F number).
- Hot, bright, busy pictures beat cold, dark, empty ones (the two cold ones and the dim Moon one are lowest).
- Collabs: the 5 posted as a collab with the channel Suit Breach are the top 5; the 2 without are the bottom 2.
  Likely the biggest single factor (they reach Suit Breach's audience too).

What doesn't:
- The Moon one (a proven Shorts topic) did worst: dim purple thumbnail, a distance number with no feel to it, no collab.
- Leaving the niche: the list video got 1.2K.

Not checked: watch time/retention (not public), the videos' insides.
