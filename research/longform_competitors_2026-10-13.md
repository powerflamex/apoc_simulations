## What they do
- **One continuous journey, 6-9 minutes, 16:9.** Not a compilation: a single "you" story that keeps escalating
  in scale ("...and couldn't stop"), each stage a new place (your room -> a dust mite -> blood -> a cell -> DNA ...;
  your street -> a bullet train -> an airliner -> the Moon in 15 s -> the Sun in 30 s).
- **Frame 0 = the question as a serif title over the scene, plus a counter** (e.g. "1.70 m" your height, top-left)
  that keeps changing the whole video. Same hook as our Shorts.
- **"You" in every title**, second person all the way.
- **"...and couldn't stop"** is the hot format right now: shrank, grew, sped up, time sped up. It works on small
  channels (469K on a 3.5K-sub channel in 8 days; Minute Zero's first 3 videos all 69K-106K within days).
- Descriptions are 2-3 lines narrating the escalation, then 3 hashtags (#whatif #science #scale).
- The second videos on the same idea (Part 1/2, "swallowed") did far worse: the first of a format wins.

## What it means for us
- Our speed-doubles Short (390K) is already the "sped up and couldn't stop" idea, and our Shorts' look matches.
  A long-form should be one escalating "you" journey with a counter, not "7 ways the world could end"-style clips.
- Be first on a format rather than copy theirs (shrink/grow/speed are taken). Candidates in the same shape:
  "What if the Earth's spin kept speeding up and couldn't stop?" (we have earth-spins), "What if you fell into the
  Sun and couldn't stop?", "What if the sea kept rising and couldn't stop?".
