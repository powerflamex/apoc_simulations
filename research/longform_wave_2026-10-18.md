## What the hits do (from frames)
1. **Flash-forward cold open, 3-6 s:** the most extreme moment with the counter at its final value and one line
   ("You can't slow down."), then a cut to the normal start with the title. All four top videos do it (MZ x2, WIA,
   WIS). MZ's Time Ran Backwards skipped it (calm street, title on frame 0) and reused the Time Sped Up street:
   264K vs 1M+.
2. **A counter that moves from the first second, plus a "next" goal under it** ("NEXT: SUNSET in 33 min",
   "FASTER THAN A BICYCLE", "NEXT: THE MOON 67,000 km"), so there's always a finish line a few seconds away.
3. **A real record broken within ~20 s** ("No human has ever run this fast." at 0:23).
4. **"You" in first person:** your legs running, your hands, your watch.
5. **Ending returns to the start** (MZ Time Sped Up ends back at 5:42 PM on the same street) or hands off to the
   next video (WIS: "Now watch what happens if you got heavier.").
6. **Visual quality is not what carries it:** MZ's two 1M+ videos are crude low-poly, and ~60% of Sped Up is
   stars on black. The premise + numbers carry it.
7. **Thumbnail = a real frame + small caps label + giant serif number + "AND STILL ..." line** (MZ's template,
   now everyone's).

## "AI slop" in the comments
- Top comment on MZ's 1.28M video: "give claude a break bro" (451 likes). 4-10% of top-level comments on MZ and WIA
  videos say AI/Claude/slop; "a dozen other such channels with same animations and text" (7 likes); on LAB: "exhausted
  of these claude slop videos they all literally have the exact same variant of title/thumbnail" (26 likes); BP
  gets "You copied this from minute zero".
- Owner's Shorts: 1-3% of comments mention AI, with almost no likes (black hole 1.2%, speed 3.2%).
- It hasn't stopped views: the videos with the most AI comments are the biggest. The risk is being seen as one of
  the clone channels, and YouTube's monetization rules against mass-produced/repetitive content.
