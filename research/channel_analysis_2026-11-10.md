# Whole-channel analysis, Nov 10, 2026

Everything here comes from the exact numbers behind each video's own Studio page (read Nov 9; 40 Shorts with a full retention curve,
8 long videos) and from watching every video's rendered file against its curve (frames at 16 moments per Short, 30 per long video).
Data and scripts: `research/data_2026-11-09/`. Ranking: `research/shorts_ranking_2026-11-10.md`. Pictures: `research/pictures_2026-11-10/`.

How sure each point is, is written next to it. "Link" numbers are rank correlations: 0 = no link, 1 = perfect.

## 1. Where the channel stands

- 26,129 subscribers. 14.4M Shorts views in all; 11.7M views and +19,748 subs in the last 28 days (Oct 12 - Nov 8).
- **Five Shorts are 68% of all views** (black hole passed 4.0M, trillion years 2.1M, speed doubles 1.9M, new asteroid 1.1M, women vanish 674K).
  Even now, 53% of a day's Shorts views come from Shorts posted before Oct 20.
- Daily views: 821K on Oct 12, down to 161K on Oct 25, back up to 360-460K on Nov 5-8. So the dip is over, but the channel is at
  about half its mid-October level.
- Viewers are almost all new or occasional (2.53M new, 396K casual, 0 "regular"); 96.7% of watch time is from people who aren't subscribed.
- Subscribers gained per 1,000 views fell from 1.5-3.8 (Oct 4-17) to 0.4-1.2 (November).
- **Ads:** the Earn page still shows Nov 4 data: 6.6M of 10M qualified Shorts views, 1,613 of 4,000 watch hours. The 6.6M matches
  "engaged views" on that date, and engaged views are now ~7.65M and growing ~200K a day, so **10M should come around Nov 21 (Nov 19-24)**
  if daily views hold. (Estimate: assumes qualified = engaged views.) The watch-hours route is far behind and won't get there first.

## 2. What goes with views (all 40 Shorts)

- **How many are still watching at 0:15-0:30.** Link with views now: 0:10 0.44, 0:15 0.59, 0:20 0.61, 0:30 0.59, 0:40 0.53.
  For Shorts posted Oct 11 or later (fair first days), 0:15 is a little ahead of 0:30 (0.48 vs 0.36 for views at 24 h; 0.60 vs 0.54 at 7 days),
  but with 32 videos that gap is inside the noise. **The 0:30 rule stays**; 0:15 is the same signal read earlier.
- **Not** stayed-to-watch (0.12), not the % at 0:03 (-0.07), not the % at the end (0.11), not brightness.
- By bucket (median views now): under 55% at 0:30: 49K; 55-70%: 107K; 70-72%: 359K; 72%+: 1.9M.
- **Inside the 45-55 s Shorts alone (11 videos), nothing in the curve predicts views.** Star picker (117K on day 1) and tornado (66K)
  sit at opposite ends of the curve; electrons got 92K with 53% at 0:30. With a decent curve, the rest is the topic. Sure about the
  numbers, not sure about the reason: 11 videos is few.
- **The first hours tell you the day:** views at 3 h predict the 24 h number at 0.82, at 6 h 0.85, at 12 h 0.96. And 24 h predicts 7 days at 0.93.
- **Growth after day 1** (7-day views / 24 h views) goes with the middle and late hold, not the opening: 0:30-0:45 0.57, hold from 0:20 to 0:30 0.63;
  early points ~0. So the opening gets the first day, the middle decides whether it keeps going. Medium sure (24 videos).
- **YouTube's first push got smaller:** first-hour views were 2.5K-16.5K for Shorts posted Oct 11-20 and 445-3,416 since Oct 21.
- Shorts whose views come from channel pages do worse (-0.58 to -0.79); those fed by the Shorts feed do better. That's a result, not a lever.

## 3. The first 10 seconds (picture: `1_first_10_seconds.jpg`)

- **Every Short's steepest fall is at 0:04-0:07.** From 0:03 to 0:10 the good ones lose 20-29 points, the weak ones 34-44.
- Is that just loopers leaving (curves start at 120-155%)? Tested: the starting bump tracks the end-of-video % at 0.90 (so it is loopers),
  but it explains little of the early loss (0.27), and the early loss still goes with views after taking it out (-0.61 across all 40).
  So the early fall is real. Fairly sure.
- **What is on screen when it holds:**
  - the outcome is hidden and a countdown is racing toward it (black hole passed, Sun vanishes: lose ~23, 0:30 at 76%, 2.2M median views; only 2 videos);
  - the picture is clearly a different one every few seconds: a new place, a new event (star picker, fall through the Earth, women vanish,
    new asteroid, trillion years), or a constant stream of named comparisons (speed doubles).
- **What is on screen when it falls:** one fixed street shot, the crowd's backs, and the danger fully visible in the sky from frame 0, only
  getting slowly bigger: tornado (-41), rogue planet (-41), metal rod (-39), Moon explodes (-38), and the older tsunami (-40), Yellowstone, Jupiter, earthquake.
  Also dark, busy night scenes with many cuts (Gojo -44, black hole appears -40, punch -38, time stop -37).
  In the 49 s era the "grows in the sky" group loses a median 39 points there against 33 for the event videos.
- Measured from the pictures: how different the frame at 0:07 is from frame 0 goes with holding from 0:03 to 0:10 at 0.54 (32 Shorts) and 0.79
  (the 11 short ones). **But it does not predict day-1 views inside the short ones (0.17), and the "grows" group's first days are no worse
  (median 62K vs 50K).** So: this explains the curve; that fixing it lifts views is likely (the curve tracks views across all 40) but not proven.
- The "danger comes at you with a finish line" rule is fine as a topic rule. The way it has been filmed is the problem: the viewer has seen the
  whole picture by 0:03. Something from the danger has to land by 0:05-0:07 (first rubble hits, first building goes, the camera moves).

## 4. The middle, 0:13 to 0:30 (picture: `2_middle_0-13_to_0-30.jpg`)

- **Goes flat** (loses ~11-14 points in 17 s): one person or one eye-level place stays in frame while the scale changes around it
  (grow a trillion times, rain for 1,000 years, fall through the Earth, trillion years).
- **Bleeds** (loses 23-36 points): the camera leaves for a far, hazy, aerial or empty view with nobody in it. Antimatter goes to a glow seen
  from space at 0:18-0:30 (76% -> 39%, the worst on the channel), oxygen to pale haze at 0:21-0:25, friction stays on one far aerial street
  (72% -> 49%), the earthquake leaves for space at 0:30, the space station sits in black space.
- More hard cuts between 0:15 and 0:30 go with a worse hold and fewer views (-0.34 to -0.59). Medium sure.
- A repeated idea a day later: big crunch had nearly the same pictures as trillion years the day before: 116K against 2.1M. One case.

## 5. The November change, and the last three

- Since the switch to 45-50 s Shorts with a finish line (Oct 31 on), the typical first day is ~50K against ~26K for the 60 s+ Shorts of
  Oct 18 - Nov 1, and the share of brand-new viewers went from 9-11% to 18%. Topics changed at the same time, so the length alone isn't proven.
- Best of the new format: star picker, 98% at 0:10, 70% at 0:30, 117K on day 1 (the best first day since Oct 17), 161K now.
- **The last three are down:** nuke 37K at 24 h; world freezes 20.9K at 12 h and 22.9K at 18 h (nearly stopped); sand grain 12.1K at 6 h
  and 15.6K at 12 h (slowed sharply). Their curves weren't readable yet (the Studio window has to be open on screen for that).
- What the pictures show (`3_newest_two.jpg`): they are the two palest openings on the channel, and sand grain is close to a blank white
  screen from about 0:09 to 0:30. By section 4 that should bleed in the middle. That's a prediction to check against the curves, not a finding.

## 7. What to do with it

1. **New Shorts: plan the 0:04-0:10 stretch on purpose.** By 0:07 the picture must be clearly a different one from frame 0 (a hit lands,
   a new place, the camera moves), or the outcome is hidden behind a racing countdown. A thing in the sky that is "a bit bigger" doesn't count.
2. **Middle: stay with the person or at eye level.** No far hazy aerials, no views from space, no near-blank screens between 0:13 and 0:30;
   keep hard cuts there few.
3. **Topic still decides the size** once the curve is decent. Keep varying the formula; don't post a near-twin of yesterday's video.
4. **Read a new Short at 3 h and 12 h.** By 12 h its first day is known.
5. **Long videos:** a new place every ~30-45 s and a counter heading to an end point. Set each long video as the "Related video" on its Short.
   Don't expect long videos to unlock ads: the Shorts route is ~11 days away.
6. Not answerable yet: why 7 Ways got 10x the reach; whether the posting hour or two-a-day matters (no visible effect, tiny sample).
