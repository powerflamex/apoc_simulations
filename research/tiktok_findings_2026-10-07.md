## The format everyone copies (Lunar's)
Serif question at the top, small-caps counter label + big number on the left, italic caption lower middle,
one continuous first-person shot (0-2 cuts), 80 s long, quiet start that builds, quiet ending, dark end card
with the question and one fact line. Nobody loops: first and last frame differ in every competitor video.
**Our seamless loops are the one thing no one else has.**

## What separates hits from flops (same format, same topics)
Same topic, 10-1000x difference: sun disappears/explodes 11.4M (Lunar) vs 719K vs 97K; oxygen gone 5 s 2.0M vs 391K;
Yellowstone 1.2M vs 3.8K; asteroid in the ocean 252K vs 70K (ours) vs 7K. Execution decides.

1. **Frame 0 is readable:** the question AND a number are on screen from the first frame, high contrast.
   Flops: faint title on a bright hazy sky (fathom Yellowstone 3.8K), or no title/counter on frame 0.
   **Our posted black hole and asteroid had no title and no counter on frame 0** (title faded in over ~1 s,
   counter appeared ~5 s). Fixed in the current versions.
2. **The number is already racing at frame 0:** Moon 238,900 -> 111,530 mi in 8 s; black hole 93,000,000 ->
   75,900,000 mi; "electrons disappear in 0:07.000" in milliseconds; air pressure falling from 14.70 psi.
   Static or slow counters do worse (Jupiter 281K: counter frozen for 5 s; supervolcano 40K: slow).
3. **Something visible happens within ~1-8 s:** bubbles at 1 s (oxygen 2.0M), everyone vanishes at 5 s (1.7M),
   pulsar beam at 3 s (1.7M), white-out at 8 s (electrons 2.8M). Our posted ones: nothing until much later.
4. **Bright, busy, human scenes:** street crossing full of people, packed beach, lit night beach with a Ferris
   wheel. Viewers see themselves there. Dim or empty scenes and a character's back blocking the view do worse.
5. **Engagement tracks reach:** hits have 9-15% likes per view, flops 1-4%. Ours: black hole 8.8% (113K),
   asteroid 4.7%, earth falls 4.1%.
6. **Captions put "you" in a concrete place with one hard fact:** "You're in Central Park when every...",
   "You're 20 miles away when...", "At the equator the ground under your feet moves east at 1,037 mph".
7. **Length: 80 s** for almost every hit. Very different styles (cartoon text-heavy POV with 16 cuts, 33K) flop.

## What this means for our videos
- Keep: question + racing counter on frame 0 (done in the new versions), the seamless loop, 60-80 s.
- Open on a bright, populated scene or the climax (earth-falls cold open did best on YouTube: 59.8% stayed).
- Make something visibly happen in the first few seconds, not just the counter.
- Captions: "You're on/at <place> when..." + one hard number.
- No big logo on screen.
- Next topics with proof: Earth stops spinning (biggest hit in the niche; nobody does it on YouTube in this
  style), Moon crashes into Earth, "X disappears for 5 seconds".
