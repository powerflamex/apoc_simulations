# IQ 1,000x voiceover (the problem-solving version): read this out loud

**How to record (same as last time, which sounded good):**
- Phone voice recorder, airplane mode. No sock, no blanket. Closet with clothes, or a small room with a bed and
  curtains. Fans and AC off if you can.
- Hold the phone at your cheek, a hand's width away, beside the corner of your mouth.
- **One file, start to finish.** Mess up? Stop, breathe, and say that whole sentence again from its start. I find every
  retake and keep your last one.
- A short breath between lines. Where it says **[wait 3 seconds]**, stay quiet for 3 seconds.
- Talk like you're telling a friend something crazy. Short lines, no rush. I place every line at its own moment.
- When you're done, send me the file (or put it in `longform/iq-1000/` named `voice.m4a`).

### 1. The opening

In four minutes, you'll be a thousand times smarter than anyone who ever lived.

### 2. It starts

You just took an IQ test on iqmaxx dot ing. A hundred. Exactly average.

Now it starts doubling.

A Rubik's cube? Three seconds.

You're the smartest person on Earth.

And something strange happens. The world slows down.

The guy who hustles everyone at chess?

Checkmate.

### 3. The ball

Here comes the rain. Even the rain is slow.

You understand every language now, just from listening.

Then you see it.

The kid's going to lose his ball. He'll chase it into the street. And that car won't stop in time.

You saw it three seconds early. For you, that's twenty seconds.

Enough time to walk over...

**[wait 3 seconds]**

...and catch it.

Nobody even saw you do it.

### 4. The hardest problem

Your mind is racing now.

The Riemann hypothesis. Unsolved for over a hundred and sixty years. A million-dollar prize.

Solved.

The pigeons take off. You can count every wingbeat.

### 5. Stuck

But your body doesn't speed up.

You try to say hi to the kid. One word takes more than ten seconds.

Every guitar string, swinging in slow motion.

### 6. The cure

So you think instead. About the thing that kills ten million people a year.

Cancer.

A protein that hunts down cancer cells, and nothing else.

Done.

And the sound is sinking. Voices turn into a rumble. Then...

**[wait 3 seconds]**

Silence.

### 7. Frozen

The rain isn't falling anymore. It's just hanging there.

You know this street so well, you can picture it from anywhere.

### 8. A star

Then, something moves.

Lightning. Crawling down the sky.

**[wait 3 seconds]**

And watching it, you figure out how to build a star.

Fusion. Clean energy, for everyone, forever.

### 9. Mars

Then you design the rocket that takes us to Mars.

And the path to get it there.

### 10. The cost

But all this thinking costs something.

Your brain normally runs on twenty watts. Now it's burning ten thousand.

Like ten space heaters inside your head.

And the kid, still waiting for his ball.

### 11. Everything

A thousand times smarter than anyone. And now you understand everything.

Why the universe exists. What came before the Big Bang. All of it.

A soap bubble takes half a minute to pop.

Your brain is at thirty-eight degrees. In seventeen more minutes, it cooks. In real time, that's one second.

**[wait 2 seconds]**

You could stay up here, alone, knowing everything. Or you could let go.

### 12. Ten years later

**[wait 3 seconds]**

You write it all down. And ten years later...

The city runs on your fusion rings.

Cancer is something people used to get.

And your rocket is leaving for Mars.

### 13. The end

And your IQ? Back to a hundred. Want to know your real one? Take the test at iqmaxx dot ing.

So, what would you do with a thousand times your IQ? Tell me in the comments.
