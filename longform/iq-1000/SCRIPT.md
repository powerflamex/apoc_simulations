# IQ 1,000x voiceover: read this out loud

**How to record (same as the Big Crunch, which sounded good):**
- Phone voice recorder, airplane mode. No sock, no blanket. Stand in a closet with clothes hanging around you, or a
  small room with a bed and curtains. Fans and AC off if you can.
- Hold the phone at your cheek, a hand's width away, beside the corner of your mouth (not straight in front).
- **One file, start to finish.** Mess up? Stop, breathe, and say that whole sentence again from its start. Don't
  restart the file; I find every retake and keep your last one.
- A short breath between lines. Where it says **[wait 3 seconds]**, stay quiet for 3 seconds.
- Easiest: play `longform/out/iq-1000_READ-ALONG.mp4` on the PC and read each line as it comes up.
- Talk like you're telling a friend something crazy. Short lines, no rush. I place every line at its own moment.
- When you're done, put the file in this folder (`longform/iq-1000/`, named `voice.m4a`) or just tell me where it is.

### 1. The opening

In four minutes, you'll be a thousand times smarter than anyone who ever lived.

### 2. It starts

You just took an IQ test on iqmaxx dot ing. A hundred. Exactly average.

Now it starts doubling, and your brain speeds up with it.

Smarter than Einstein.

The smartest person on Earth.

And the world... slows down.

Look at them. Everyone's moving in slow motion.

Here comes the rain.

Even the rain is slow.

You understand every language now, just from listening.

### 3. The ball

Then you see it.

The kid's going to lose his ball. He'll chase it into the street. And that car won't stop in time.

You saw it three seconds early. For you, that's twenty seconds.

Enough time to walk over...

**[wait 3 seconds]**

...and catch it.

To everyone else, you just reached out and caught a ball.

### 4. Faster

Your mind is racing now. You could crack math problems nobody has solved in a hundred years.

The pigeons take off. You can count every wingbeat.

### 5. Stuck

But your body doesn't speed up.

You try to say hi to the kid. One word takes more than ten seconds.

Even moving your eyes takes seconds.

Every guitar string, swinging in slow motion.

### 6. Silence

And the sound is sinking. Voices turn into a rumble. Then lower. Then...

**[wait 3 seconds]**

Silence.

All you can feel is your heart. About one beat a minute.

### 7. Frozen

The rain isn't falling anymore. It's just hanging there.

And you know this street so well, you can picture it from anywhere.

### 8. Lightning

Then, something moves.

Lightning. Crawling down the sky.

**[wait 3 seconds]**

The thunder? It's still minutes away.

### 9. The heat

Every person in the city, stopped mid-step.

But all this thinking costs something.

Your brain normally runs on twenty watts. Now it's burning thousands.

And it's heating up.

The fountain, frozen in midair.

Five hundred times faster. Ten thousand watts. Like ten space heaters inside your head.

And the kid, still waiting for his ball.

### 10. A thousand times

A thousand times smarter than anyone.

One second feels like seventeen minutes.

A soap bubble pops in front of you, and it takes half a minute.

Your heart beats once every fourteen minutes.

Your brain is at thirty-eight degrees. In seventeen more minutes, it cooks. In real time, that's one second.

**[wait 2 seconds]**

You could stay up here, alone, in a frozen world. Or you could let go.

### 11. Back

**[wait 3 seconds]**

Everything you just saw took about thirty seconds.

Nobody noticed a thing. Except the kid.

And your IQ? Back to a hundred. Want to know your real one? Take the test at iqmaxx dot ing.

So, what would you do with a thousand times your IQ? Tell me in the comments.
